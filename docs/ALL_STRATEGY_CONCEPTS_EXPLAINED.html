📖 Foundation: How Funding Rates Generate Cash

Perpetual futures contracts have no expiration date. To keep futures prices anchored to the spot market price, exchanges enforce a Funding Rate payment every 8 hours (00:00, 08:00, 16:00 UTC):

  • Positive Rate (+): Bullish market. Buyers (Longs) must pay direct cash to Sellers (Shorts).
  • Negative Rate (-): Bearish market. Sellers (Shorts) must pay direct cash to Buyers (Longs).
  • The Profit Engine: By pairing equal long and short positions that neutralize 100% of price volatility (Delta-Neutral), we capture the rate spread with zero exposure to Bitcoin's price swings.
1. Cross-Exchange Funding Spread Capture (idea-03-cross-exchange-funding)
⭐ Flagship — Active Live Paper
💡 The Plain-English Idea
Different crypto exchanges (e.g., Binance vs Bybit or Delta India) frequently have different funding rates at the exact same second. We Short the high-rate exchange (to collect high payments) and Long the low-rate exchange (paying little to nothing).
💰 How Exactly Do We Get Profits? (3-Step Flow)
1. Zero Price Risk: Buy $5,000 BTC on Bybit while Selling $5,000 BTC on Binance. If BTC drops $5,000, Bybit loses $500 but Binance gains $500. Net price impact = $0.00.
2. The Spread Gap: At the 8h funding clock (08:00 UTC):
  • Binance (+0.06% rate): As a Short, you RECEIVE +$3.00.
  • Bybit (+0.01% rate): As a Long, you PAY -$0.50.
  • Gross Funding Payout: +$3.00 - $0.50 = +$2.50 Net Profit per 8h (~27% APY on $10k capital).
3. The 9.5-Minute Sniper Execution: The bot enters 5.0 minutes before 08:00 UTC (when the rate is already fixed) and exits 4.5 minutes after. You only stay in the trade for 9.5 minutes!
⚠️ Possibilities for Loss & How the Bot Protects You
Loss Scenario What Causes the Potential Loss? How the Bot Prevents / Mitigates It
1. Fee Drag (Fees > Spread) Taker fees on both exchanges exceed the funding rate gap, causing a net negative payout after opening & closing. Strict Spread Hurdle: Bot requires Spread ≥ 0.035%. It will NEVER open a trade unless net profit after all taker fees & slippage is guaranteed positive.
2. Leg-Fill Latency (Leg Lag) Binance fills in 20ms, but Bybit takes 400ms. In those 380ms, Bitcoin moves $100 against the unhedged open leg. Concurrent Async Sockets + 100ms Timeout: Bot fires both orders simultaneously. If leg 2 is not filled in 100ms, leg 1 is immediately cancelled/aborted at market.
3. Basis Drift in 9.5 min During the 9.5 minutes of holding, Binance perp and Bybit perp prices diverge slightly (e.g. Bybit falls $15 more than Binance). Ultra-Short Holding Window: Holding for only 9.5 minutes around settlement limits statistical basis variance to under 0.005%, far smaller than the captured spread.
4. Exchange API Disconnect One exchange's API freezes or drops connection while holding open positions. Desync Watchdog: 24/7 background monitor checks heartbeats every 500ms. If connection drops >2s, it instantly closes the opposite leg or engages emergency fallback.
2. Spot-Perpetual Cash-and-Carry (idea-01-cash-and-carry)
Audited Phase B Complete
💡 The Plain-English Idea
Buy actual crypto on the Spot market and simultaneously open an equal-sized Short Perpetual Futures contract on the exact same exchange (e.g., Binance).
💰 How Exactly Do We Get Profits? (Continuous Yield)
1. 100% Price Immunity: You own 1 BTC on spot and are short 1 BTC on futures. If Bitcoin drops from $60,000 to $30,000, your spot loses $30k but your short gains $30k. Your dollar balance is unchanged.
2. The Funding Cashflow: In normal/bullish markets, the funding rate is positive (e.g., +0.03% every 8h):
  • As a Short on futures, the exchange deposits $1.50 into your account 3 times a day (+$4.50/day).
  • Over 30 days on a $10,000 portfolio: $4.50 × 30 = +$135.00 gross yield.
  • After $12.00 roundtrip trading fees = +$123.00 Net Monthly Profit (~15% to 28% APY).
⚠️ Possibilities for Loss & How the Bot Protects You
Loss Scenario What Causes the Potential Loss? How the Bot Prevents / Mitigates It
1. Negative Funding Bleed Market crashes into a prolonged bear regime and funding turns negative (-0.03%). Since you are Short, you must pay $4.50/day. Auto-Unwind Threshold: If 8h funding drops below +0.005%, the bot automatically sells spot BTC, closes the short, and parks 100% in stablecoins earning 5-8% APY.
2. Short Liquidation on Rally Using high isolated leverage (e.g. 10x): A sudden 12% vertical rally wipes out the short margin before spot profits can be transferred. 1x Leverage + Portfolio Margin: The bot uses 1x leverage and enables Unified Portfolio Margin, where the spot Bitcoin itself acts as collateral for the short (impossible to liquidate).
3. Basis Compression at Exit You enter when Futures is trading $50 above Spot, but exit when Futures is $30 below Spot (losing $80 on price basis convergence). Basis Premium Entry Filter: The bot only opens when the futures premium (basis) is historically elevated, and only unwinds when the basis returns to neutral.
4. Borrow Interest Spike If borrowing USDT or crypto for margin spot legs, borrow interest rates on the exchange can suddenly jump to 30%+ APY. Cash-Collateralized Spot: The bot uses 100% equity-funded spot purchases (zero borrowed margin loans).
3. Bounded "Rubber-Band" Mean-Reversion Spread Model (idea-rs-ou-mean-reversion)
Statistical Arbitrage • Audited
💡 The Plain-English Idea
Think of the funding rate difference between two exchanges like a stretched rubber band. When excitement or panic pushes the spread unusually wide, it almost always snaps back to normal within 2 to 12 hours.
💰 How Exactly Do We Get Profits? (The Double Harvest Engine)
1. Dual Profit Streams: In this model, you make money in TWO ways at the same time:
  • Stream A (Funding Payments): You collect the high funding rate payment from the expensive exchange while holding the hedge.
  • Stream B (Price Convergence Gain): When a rate blows out to +0.08%, that exchange's futures price trades at a temporary premium ($60,050 vs $60,000). When the rubber band snaps back to normal, the price gap closes, giving you an extra trading gain!
2. Concrete Example: An anomaly pushes OKX rate to +0.08% vs Binance at +0.01% (0.07% spread). Bot shorts OKX ($5k) and longs Binance ($5k). Four hours later, the gap normalizes back to 0.01%. You pocket +$3.50 funding + +$4.20 price convergence = +$7.70 Net Profit in 4 Hours.
⚠️ Possibilities for Loss & How the Bot Protects You
Loss Scenario What Causes the Potential Loss? How the Bot Prevents / Mitigates It
1. Spread Blowout (Regime Shift) Instead of snapping back, the spread widens further (e.g. blows out to 0.15% due to an exchange liquidity crisis). Statistical Stop-Loss at |Z| ≥ 3.5: If the spread expands beyond 3.5 standard deviations, the bot automatically cuts both legs at market, capping maximum loss to <0.15%.
2. Half-Life Expansion (Stagnation) The spread doesn't blow out, but stays wide for 3-4 days without snapping back, tying up capital. Automated 48-Hour Time-Stop: If the spread does not mean-revert to the target exit zone within 48 hours, the bot automatically unwinds both positions.
3. Fee Drag on Small Spikes Entering when the spread spike is too small (e.g. 0.02%), meaning roundtrip trading fees eat all the convergence profit. Z-Score Entry Filter (|Z| ≥ 2.0): The bot strictly requires the spread anomaly to exceed 2.0 standard deviations (spread ≥ 0.045%), ensuring profit is ≥ 3× fees.
4. Paired Leg Fill Latency One leg fills instantly, but the second leg lags during extreme volatility. Atomic Async Routing: Bot sends simultaneous IOC limit/market orders with a 100ms cancellation timeout.
4. Rate-Momentum Sizing (idea-02-rate-momentum)
Directional Tilt • Audited
💡 The Plain-English Idea
A trend-following strategy with an "anti-crowding brake". When a trend is healthy, the bot takes full size. When retail FOMO pushes funding to extreme highs, the bot automatically cuts position size so you don't get wiped out in sudden liquidation cascades.
